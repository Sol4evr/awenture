# Formal-paper zero-overlap rule

For original-paper mode, overlap is defined at two levels:

1. Source overlap: the same source PDF fingerprint cannot appear twice.
2. Question overlap: the same formal question identity cannot appear in more than one formal paper, including cross-year aliases or duplicated paper variants.

A formal paper is publishable only when all of its question identities and answer-key entries are bound to one source-year paper and the aggregate set intersection against every other published formal paper is empty.

Generated Daily Practice and Bonus Challenge are outside this rule because they are not formal original-paper forms; they remain governed by their existing novelty/cooldown rules.
